STYLE (use for every image, identical across the whole set):

I am making a set of 102 square illustrations for the cards of a historical strategy game (1450–1800). They must all look like one consistent set.

- Look: hand-coloured woodcut / early-modern book engraving. Bold black iron-gall ink linework with cross-hatching, then flat watercolour washes.
- Palette only: ink black, sepia brown, warm parchment, oxblood red (#9e1f2a), lapis blue (#2f5d8a), verdigris green (#3e7a3e), ochre (#c49b3a). No other colours, no gradients, no glow.
- Composition: ONE clear subject, large and centred, readable as a small thumbnail (about 80 px wide in the game). Simple silhouette, strong contrast. Slight three-quarter view for people and units.
- Background: plain warm parchment (#f1e8d4), flat, no scenery beyond a hint of ground or sea.
- Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, captions, borders, frames or watermarks.
- Format: square PNG, 1024 × 1024.
- Keep line weight, colouring and lighting the same in every image of the set.
