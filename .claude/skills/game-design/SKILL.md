# Lasting Empires — game design rules

## Pillars
1. **Everything is a card.** Every player action is spent from a card. New actions get a card definition in `src/engine/cards.ts` (category, kind, weight function). Never add a free button that bypasses cards, except declaring war, moving troops, setting what a workshop makes, closing a trade route and dismissing/disbanding. Buttons that need a card stay visible and say which card they need.
   - **Card types** (`cardType`): People (hire someone), Actions (missions and orders), and in Military also Troops (by kind: Levy, Infantry, Ranged, Cavalry, Artillery; each hires the best unit of its kind researched, see `UNIT_LINES`) and Improvements (equipment, Drill Sergeant). Elite units come from Forge recipes.
   - **Getting cards:** free draws each quarter, or the Cards tab shop: buy a card of one type with nation points (`buyCard(s, cat, type)`: 3 shown, keep 1; a type with one card in the deck is given straight away).
   - **Permanent cards** (stay after use, the mission costs ducats): only Embassy and Peace Envoy. Everything else is used up.
   - **Identical cards stack**, and the hand has a limit by realm rank (see Hand limit). Wars give March Orders (3 to the attacker, 2 to the defender); at peace only 2 are kept.
2. **People decide quality.** A mission card needs a person of the right role. The person's **rarity** (Common → Legendary) sets the outcome odds (`OUTCOME_ODDS` in `src/data/characters.ts`) and trait strength (`traitMult`). New missions must roll an outcome with `rollOutcome` (or `rollBest` when a core card grants advantage) and scale their result by it. Show the outcome with `outcomeItem` in the reveal.
3. **Surprises are revealed, not hidden.** Results arrive as face-down cards that flip (`push(s, { kind: 'reveal', ... })`). History events are known in advance (timeline) but their outcomes branch.
4. **Middle by default.** Core cards (`src/data/core.ts`) move the realm off the centre of an axis. Every core card has real pros and cons.
5. **Three currencies, kept distinct.** Manpower = 1 per troop or person hired. Nation points = buying cards and core cards. Ducats = upkeep, salaries, imports, construction.

## Where things live
- Data only: `src/data/*` (no logic beyond small pure helpers).
- Rules: `src/engine/*` — pure functions on `GameState`, seeded RNG via `rand(s)`. Never use `Math.random` in the engine (except picking a seed in `newGame`).
- UI: `src/ui/*` — reads state, calls engine functions, never mutates state directly except through engine calls.
- New saved fields must be added to `GameState` in `src/types.ts` and initialised in `newGame`. Old saves get them through a numbered step: run `npm run make-save` (it stores a save of the current version in `scripts/saves/`), raise `SAVE_VERSION` in `src/engine/turn.ts`, and add `MIGRATIONS[oldVersion]`. Never edit a step that has shipped. `npm test` loads every stored save.

## Systems map
- Navy: `src/engine/navy.ts` (ships live in `s.fleet` / `nation.fleet`; ship cards have kind `ship`; hiring with a general makes an admiral). Sea battles reuse `battle.ts` with `naval: true` and a `follow` step (land attack or land defence).
- Peace and subjects: `src/engine/diplo.ts` (war score from `War.gains*` and `wins*`; terms spend score; `overlord`/`subject` on `Nation`).
- Society: `src/engine/society.ts` (province `culture`/`religion`, `happinessOf`, yearly stability drift; `s.art`, `s.taxBurden`, `s.accepted`).

## Research
- Technologies live in `TECHS` (`src/data/techs.ts`), about 4 per field per age, each with `age`, `cost` and plain effects `fx` (income, army, battle, research, control, happiness, routes, card rates…) added up by `techFx(s, key)`. Prefer a new `fx` key over a one-off `hasTech` check. No research beyond the current age (`missionBlocked`).
- Buildings list their benefits in `gives`; the build screen shows Cost (ducats, materials, time, upkeep) beside Benefit. Unresearched buildings are not offered.

## Start dates
- `newGame(nation, seed, startYear)`: later starts build the 1451 world, run `initSociety`, then `applyStart` (owners, faith, realms, subjects, wars, age) and `setupLaterPlayer`. Ownership lives in `src/data/later-owners.ts`, generated by `scripts/merge-starts.py`; per-date tuning in `src/data/starts.ts`. Anything indexed by age must have six entries (`AGE_STATE_COST`).

## Battles: what the player decides
- The generals pick the tactics (`aiPick`); the player's choices are the reserve (`setReserve` before round 1, `commitReserve` later), and the withdrawal (`retreat` after round 1 or 2). A new battle decision gets an engine function with a `can…` test, a button in `showBattle`, a line in the Rules tab and a check in `scripts/test.ts`; `autoResolve` must still finish any battle with no choices.
- Ground is data: `TERRAINS` in `src/data/terrain.ts`, applied once by `ground()` in `battle.ts`. `terrainOf(p)` derives it; set a province by hand in `TERRAIN_OVERRIDES`. A new kind of ground is a row there and nothing else. Ground applies only to battles the player fights (`createBattle`); a rule that hinders every attacker in the world changes how fast rivals grow, so simulate before widening it.
- A general's trait may favour tactics (`TRAIT_TACTIC` in `src/data/troops.ts`): that is how the player reads an enemy. A new general trait should say what it favours.
- Lent companies carry `ally` on the `BattleUnit`; anything that writes battle results back must leave them to `syncAllies`.

## Battles: command and walls
- A commander leads at most `commandOf(skill)` companies (`src/data/characters.ts`): ordinary 3, good 4, rare 5, epic 6, legendary 8. Unnamed enemy commanders lead `GENERIC_COMMAND` (4), one more at their capital. Extra troops stay for the next battle, so big armies take several assaults.
- Walls: each fort level adds a garrison unit that takes the first blows and +25% defence. The highest level comes from `maxFort(s)` (`FORT_BY_AGE`: 2 in the Age of Crusade up to 5; Star Forts +1). Never raise `fort` without clamping to `maxFort`.
- Historic rulers get their talent from traits (`histSkill` in `turn.ts`): Able +2, Warrior +1, Ambitious +1.
- Conquest only occupies (`occupiedFrom`). The peace treaty decides: `keep:<province>` terms keep occupations, the rest goes back (`concludePeace`). A realm left with no land waits at the table (AI skips it) and dies only if the treaty leaves it nothing.
- A beaten realm (no army, heartland or capital held) asks for peace each quarter (`autoSurrender` pushes a `peace` prompt with `surrender`): the player sets any terms.

## Diplomacy odds
- `diploChance` (`src/engine/missions.ts`): base by kind + diplomat skill + relations/250 + `powerEdge` (−15%…+25% from army + 3 per province, log scale). Capped at 95%, or 99% at relations 80+ (`diploCap`). A near-certain deal (≥90%) or an embassy to a non-hostile realm never gets a bad twist.
- Courts hold 7 (`courtCap`); realms start with 6. Hire cards are not drawn while the court is full, and permanent cards you already hold are never drawn (`randomCard`).

## Missions and government
- Mission mode: `MISSIONS` in `src/data/missions.ts`; goal kinds in `Goal` (`src/types.ts`), measured by `goalView` in `src/engine/goals.ts` (title, status in words, progress). A new goal kind needs a `goalView` case and, for custom missions, a `GOAL_KINDS` entry in the builder.
- Governments: `GOVS` with `fx` like research; changing needs the Revolution card and `startRevolution`.

## Hand limit
- `HAND_LIMITS` by rank (per deck, in all); permanent cards and burdens do not count (`countsToLimit`). Draws never fail: over the limit the player sells extras (`sellCards`) before End Turn; `trimHand` is the engine's safety net and the sim's choice.

## Missions
- Data in `src/data/missions.ts` (`MISSIONS`): start year, nation, `goals`, optional `beginner`, `wild`, extra `cards`/`gold`, and guided `steps` (from the `STEPS` record).
- Goals are checked every quarter by `checkGoals` in `src/engine/goals.ts`; `goalView` shows each goal's progress. A new goal kind needs a type in `src/types.ts`, a case in `goalView`, and a baseline flag in `newMissionGame` if it counts gains (`goal_*0`, `stat_*`).
- In mission mode the mission alone decides victory: finishing it shows the celebration screen and the player may play on. Rank only matters in the sandbox.
- Unlocks: beginner missions first; finishing them all opens the historic and wild missions; one real mission opens the sandbox (`realUnlocked`, `sandboxUnlocked`, saved in localStorage).
- Beginner missions teach one system each and are guided: steps complete in order, the current step leads the timeline, and the generic tutorial is off (`tutorial = -1`). Every step must be reachable with the cards the mission hands out.
- Verify a mission with the sim (`START=<year> MISSION=<id> NATIONS=<nation> npm run sim`; set START for 1600/1700 missions) or a scripted play when the bot cannot follow steps. Check every step's cards are handed out (step cards add copies), and that no goal is already met at the start.

## Card rates
- Six decks: Military, Diplomacy, Internal, Economy, Research, People (`culture`). Each draws `0.5 + bonuses` cards a year (capped at 1.4); fractions carry over (`cardAccum`). Add bonuses through `cardRates` parts (buildings, research, monarch, cities, core cards) so the breakdown stays visible to the player.
- The hand limit (below), the yearly mission cap, army size and building slots are the limits.

## Events, people and stories
- An event is an `EventDef` (`src/engine/eventkit.ts`): text, options with `can` (why it is blocked) and `run` (returns what to reveal). Use the kit's helpers (`good`, `bad`, `neutral`, `stab`, `needs`, `pay`, `scaled`); never write a bare number of ducats. Follow `docs/WRITING.md` (60 words, speak to "you", one concrete detail, every option costs something).
- Where it goes: history and general events in `events.ts` / `annals.ts`; events about people in `court.ts` (wrap with `courtEvent` so the court stirs at most once a year); stories in three steps in `chains.ts`.
- A choice that comes back: `later(s, id, min, max, vars)` from an option; the later step has `chance: 0` and reads `vars.path`. Three steps at most, and every road must end. `npm test` walks every road and renders every text.
- People: read loyalty and wishes with `loyaltyOf(c)` / `wishOf(c)` and change loyalty with `addLoyalty(c, d)` (`court.ts`); never read `c.loyalty` directly (it is unset until something changes it). A new source of loyalty gets a line in the Rules tab text if the player should know it.
- Rival generals: `commanderOf(s, nation) ?? genericGeneral(s)` when building the enemy side of a battle against the player.
- Lines of the log that a history should keep get a pattern and a weight in `WEIGHTS` (`chronicle.ts`).
- Avoid dice where a hash will do (`appoint`, `loyaltyOf`): new systems should not shift the world's other rolls.

## Pace and difficulty
- `src/engine/pace.ts` is pure (no RNG, no changes). `attention(s)` lists what is still open this turn; `stopReasons` says when skip-ahead must stop. A new thing the player should not miss gets a rule in `attention` (with an id that stays the same while the thing is open) and a check in `scripts/test.ts`; a new kind of mission card needs a case in `cardHasTarget`.
- Never list something the player cannot act on, and never list permanent cards: a list that nags is a list that is ignored.
- Difficulty is one table, `DIFFICULTY` in `src/data/difficulty.ts`, read through `diff(s)`. Standard must stay the game as balanced; a new lever gets a neutral value there (×1 or +0) and is used as `diff(s).lever`, never as a number in the logic.
- `newGame(nation, seed, startYear, { difficulty, length })`; `length` is a `LENGTHS` id and only shortens an open game.

## Balance workflow
See `docs/BALANCE.md` for targets, current results and every lever. Run `MODE=reasonable|passive|greedy|big SEEDS=5 npm run sim` and compare against the targets table before and after any change.

- Run `npm run sim` (headless bot plays 9 full campaigns, 1453–1750). It must finish with no crash.
- Watch: player rank spread, nations alive at the end, gold hoarding, hand sizes, settlement growth, veteran troops.
- Balance is tuned late; prefer adding levers (costs, weights, rates in data files) over hard-coding numbers in logic.

## Related skills
- `game-design-principles`: general theory (core loop, player motivation, flow, pacing). Check new mechanics against the 30-second loop and the reward schedules there.
- `web-games`: browser constraints (pause when the tab is hidden, audio after the first tap, small startup payload).

## Checklist for a new mechanic
1. Data in `src/data`, rules in `src/engine`, typed in `src/types.ts`, a numbered step in `MIGRATIONS`.
2. A card (or a reason it is not card-driven).
3. Outcome roll scaled by rarity, shown in a reveal.
4. Visible in the right panel (Military, Diplomacy, Internal, Economy, Research) with the reason when blocked.
5. Bot updated in `scripts/sim.ts`; `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` and `npm run sim` pass.
6. The action ticked in `docs/ROADMAP.md` if it is one of them; no new system before Phase 4.
